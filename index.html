Performance Transparency

Show evidence. Label every result.

We believe in honest reporting. Performance data is verified and clearly separated between backtests and live trading.

What a credible report includes

  • ✓Period & market: date range, symbols, timeframe and broker/exchange.
  • ✓Costs: spreads, commissions, swaps/funding and slippage.
  • ✓Risk metrics: maximum drawdown, trade count, profit factor and net return.
  • ✓Method: test settings, data source and whether results are simulated or live.

Metrics glossary

Net returnResult after stated costs
Max drawdownLargest peak-to-trough decline
Win rateWinning trades ÷ closed trades
Profit factorGross profit ÷ gross loss

Do not treat historical or simulated performance as a promise of future results.
